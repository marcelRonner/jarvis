# US-6.1 – Request and Receive Espresso

## Card

As a **Customer**, I **WOULD LIKE** to request and receive a complimentary Espresso from the ATM so that I can enjoy a free coffee during my ATM visit.

---

## Conversation

- The Customer must have an active `Session` (status `ACTIVE`) before requesting coffee — see [Authenticate](../../use_case/authenticate/authenticate.md).
- The ATM checks `CoffeeMachine.status` and `CoffeeMachine.beansRemaining` before dispensing.
- On success, the ATM creates a `BeverageOrder` (beverageType `ESPRESSO`, status `PENDING`), dispenses the coffee, and updates the `BeverageOrder` to status `COMPLETED`.
- Corresponds to **Business Process steps 4d.1 – 4d.3** and [Order Coffee activity diagram](../../use_case/order_coffee/orderCoffee.md) steps "ATM creates BeverageOrder (status = PENDING)" → "ATM updates BeverageOrder (status = COMPLETED)".
- **KANO: Attractive quality (WOULD LIKE)** — Coffee dispensing is a delighter feature; customers expect an ATM to handle cash, not beverages. Presence surprises positively; absence does not disappoint.

---

## Confirmation

**Given** a `Session` with status `ACTIVE` and a `CoffeeMachine` with status `ONLINE` and `beansRemaining > 0`,  
**When** the Customer selects "Coffee" from the transaction menu,  
**Then** the ATM creates a `BeverageOrder` with `beverageType = ESPRESSO` and `status = COMPLETED`, the `CoffeeMachine` dispenses an Espresso, `beansRemaining` is decremented by 1, and an `AuditLog` entry is written.

---

**Given** a `Session` with status `ACTIVE` and a `CoffeeMachine` with status `OFFLINE` or `OUT_OF_STOCK`,  
**When** the Customer selects "Coffee" from the transaction menu,  
**Then** the ATM displays an "out of service" message, no `BeverageOrder` is created, and the Customer is returned to the transaction selection screen.
