# CR-1.2 – Account Balance on Receipt

A functional change to receipt printing in the ATM system.

---

## Request

Requested by the owner on 2026-03-02, from the Bank's customer service: *"Most people who ask for a
receipt want to know what is left in their account. Put the balance on it."*

## Analysis

In v1.1 a Receipt holds the Transaction details only. The balance is already held on the Account
entity, so the domain model does not change. The change touches the Print Receipt use case and its
activity diagram, the Print Receipt epic and its happy-path story. The business process step 5.3
*Generate and print Receipt* stays as it is.

## Decisions

- **Every Receipt, every Transaction type** — `WITHDRAWAL`, `BALANCE_INQUIRY` and `TRANSFER` — shows
  the Account `balance`.
- **The balance is read after the Transaction**, so it includes the amount just withdrawn or
  transferred.
- **No opt-out.** A Customer who does not want the balance printed declines the Receipt, as in
  US-5.2.
- **Release** — the new story belongs to *R2 – Core Transactions*, next to US-5.1.
- **Assumption:** printing the balance on a Receipt is permitted by the Bank's privacy policy; the
  Bank confirms.
