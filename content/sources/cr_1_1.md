# CR-1.1 – Eject Card Before Cash Dispensing

A functional change to the withdrawal path of the ATM system.

---

## Request

Requested by the owner on 2026-02-22, after feedback from the Bank's branch staff: *"Customers take
the cash and walk away, and the card stays in the machine. The card should come out before the
cash."*

## Analysis

In v1.0 the Card is ejected only when the Session closes (step 5.5), after the cash has been
dispensed. The change moves the ejection forward in the withdrawal path only. It touches the
business process, the Withdraw Cash use case and its activity diagram, the Withdraw Cash epic and
its happy-path story. No entity changes: Card and Session already carry what is needed.

## Decisions

- **Withdrawal only.** A new step 4a.6 *Eject Card* comes between creating the Transaction and
  dispensing cash, which is renumbered 4a.7. Balance inquiries and transfers are unchanged.
- **The Session stays `ACTIVE`** after the Card is ejected, so the Customer can still print a
  Receipt or choose another transaction.
- **Step 5.5** becomes *Close Session, eject Card if not already ejected*.
- **Cash waits for the Card.** The CashDispenser dispenses only once the Customer has taken the Card.
- **Release** — the new story belongs to *R1 – Walking Skeleton*: a withdrawal that loses Cards is
  not releasable.
- **Assumption:** a Customer who never takes the ejected Card is out of scope for this change, as
  it was for the Card ejected at step 5.5.

## Acceptance

- After a withdrawal, the Session is still `ACTIVE`.
- Balance inquiries and transfers still eject the Card at step 5.5.
